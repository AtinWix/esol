Task 4: four short texts

Listen twice. First time: yes or no. Second time: one thing. Tell your friend.

1 Café
Yes or no? Does the man want sugar? → No, he doesn’t.
One thing: How much is the tea? → One pound eighty (£1.80).
2 Library
Yes or no? Does the woman want to join the library? → Yes, she does.
One thing: How many books can she take? → Three.
3 Phone message
Yes or no? Does the film start at eight o’clock? → No, it doesn’t (seven o’clock).
One thing: What time do they meet? → Half past six.
4 Street
Yes or no? Is the bank far? → No, it isn’t.
One thing: Where is the bank? → Next to the post office.

Look at the board
Learners: projector, listen, then tell a friend. Notebook for notes.
Say: “Task 4. Listen to the first text. First time: yes or no.” Press Play. The track plays twice. Say: “Tell your friend your two answers.” Do the four texts one after another, then press Show next answer (eight clicks). Ask for full sentences: “No, he doesn’t.” “It is one pound eighty.” Check sound before the lesson. If short of time, do texts 1 and 3 only.

Exam tips

  • Listen to the whole question. Then answer with a full sentence.
  • Task 4: first time, yes or no. Second time, one thing.
  • Task 3: I can see… There is… There are… He is …-ing.
  • Task 2: use your five key words. Talk for one minute.
  • Not clear? Say: “Sorry, can you repeat that, please?”
  • Speak slowly and clearly.
