Then write five sentences in Teams General

  1. Yesterday (past simple): Yesterday I …
  2. Now (present continuous): Now I'm …
  3. Tomorrow (a plan): Tomorrow I'm going to …
  4. Your opinion: I think … will …
  5. An offer to a friend: I'll …

Example: Yesterday I went to the market. Now I'm sitting in the PC room. Tomorrow I'm going to visit my cousin. I think it will be cold at the weekend. I'll help my friend carry her shopping.

Feedback and exam day

Your observer sheet

Which task has the fewest ticks? Hands up: Task 1? Task 2? Task 3? Task 4?

On exam day

  • Listen carefully to the assessor.
  • You can ask: Can you say that again, please?
  • Speak to your partner as well as the assessor.
  • Write notes, not sentences, in your planning minute.
  • Task 4: read the options first; main idea first, then the detail.
Look at the board
Learners: projector, then their observer sheet.
Say: "Look at your observer sheet. Which task had the fewest ticks?" Take a show of hands for each task. Board eight sentences you noted, without names; pairs correct them. Finish with the exam-day tips. Remind learners to keep Handout 1 and the worksheet.

★ Referee Interviews

Optional – for fast finishers (about 10 minutes). The lesson does not depend on it.

Groups of three: A asks, B answers in a full sentence, C is the referee. Change roles every three minutes. The referee listens for a missing helper verb, a, an or the, or a plural ending.
