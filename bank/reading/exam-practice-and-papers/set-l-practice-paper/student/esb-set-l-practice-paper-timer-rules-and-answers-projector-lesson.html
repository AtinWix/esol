Exam rules

  1. Phones away – in your bag.
  2. No talking. Eyes on your own paper.
  3. Desks apart.
  4. Blue or black pen.
  5. Answer every question – you lose no marks for a wrong answer.
  6. Read the title and the first sentences first.
  7. Finished? Check your answers. Then do the word search (Handout 5) quietly.
