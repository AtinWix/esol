    Good for a complaint email?
    
  
    Polite and clear = ✓. Rude or angry = ✗. Angry emails are easy to ignore.
    
  
    
    
    
    
    Why?
    
      
      
        
    • A manager gets many emails. Rude emails get a short answer: "Your comments have been noted."
      • 
        
    • Polite emails with facts (where, when, what happens) are hard to ignore.
      • 
        
    • Many polite emails with the same facts are very hard to ignore.
