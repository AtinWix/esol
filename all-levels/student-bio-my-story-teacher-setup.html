My Story – teacher setup

Students' answers go to your own Microsoft Form. Do this once.

  1. In Microsoft Forms, make a new form with two Text questions: Name, then Answers (set it to Long answer).
  2. Click … (top right), then Get pre-filled URL. Type x in both boxes. Click Get prefilled link and copy it.
  3. Paste it here and press the button:

No Form? Give students the plain link. At the end they press Copy and send you their answers.
