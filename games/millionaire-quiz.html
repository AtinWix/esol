    Who Wants to Be a Millionaire? – Set up

    Automatic questions only work from your Claude link. Use the prompt below with any AI instead.

    1. Copy the prompt. 2. Paste it into any AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini) and confirm the level and topic when it asks. 3. Copy the AI's whole answer and paste it into My questions – each player gets a new set.

    Questions go from very easy to very hard. 15 questions = £1 million. Safe amounts: £1,000 and £32,000.

    Ask the Audience
