Activity 1 · Hawa’s Morning

Put Hawa’s morning in order

Hawa got up at six o’clock. Before breakfast, she washed her son’s school shirt. After they ate, she took him to school. Then she caught the bus to college. She arrived late because the bus broke down on the way. At lunchtime she phoned the school to check on her son.

Which happened first? A learner says the event and the words in the text that prove it. Then tap it.

Exam routine · Ordering questions

Three steps

  1. Underline every date, month and time word in the text.
  2. Write 1, 2, 3… next to each event. Use pen – no pencil in the exam.
  3. Check: does your number 1 match the first event the exam gives you?

Exam routine · Alphabetical order

On paper in the exam

  1. Write the alphabet across the top of the page: a b c d e f g h i j k l m n o p q r s t u v w x y z
  2. Cross off each word when you use it.
  3. Count: you must have the same number of words you started with.

The exam is on paper. Today you practise on screen.

Activity 3 · ESB paper · Set L · Tasks 1 and 2 · 14 questions
40:00
Read the title and first sentences first.
  • Blue or black pen. Phones away. No talking. No dictionary.
  • Read the title and the first sentences. Read the whole text once. Then answer.
  • Answer every question. You can go back.
  • Finished early? Write the line that proves each answer.

Give the answer – then the words that prove it

One mark each. Questions 2 and 6 need both reasons. Marks out of 14.

Activity 5 · Exam day

My exam routine

  1. Read the title.
  2. Read the first sentence of each paragraph.
  3. Read everything once.
  4. Answer the easy questions first.
  5. Check each answer in the text. Never leave a question blank.
