Who is "they" / "she"?

Read the sentence before. The answer is usually a person or thing just before the word.

What does … mean?

Find the word in the text. Read the whole sentence. Try each answer in its place.

"This text…"

Ask: is it news (reports), an advert (advertises), an invitation (invites), or help (advises)?

Write 2 things

Alphabetical order

Task 1

Read the text and answer questions 1–7.

College News

Too cold to learn?

A survey of 120 students shows that most of them are unhappy about the heating at the college. Eight out of ten students said that their classroom was too cold in October.

The heating system is more than 30 years old. It often stops working early in the morning, when the building is coldest. Last week, the temperature in one classroom was only 14°C.

"We sit in our coats and scarves all day," says Maria, an ESOL student. "My hands are so cold that I can't write properly. Some of my classmates have stopped coming to class because they keep getting ill."

Teachers are also worried. They say that students find it hard to concentrate, and fewer people come to lessons on very cold days.

The survey also asked students what helps. Many bring a hot drink to class. Others try to sit near the radiators, but these are often cold too.

The college manager, Mr Patel, says the college will put in a new heating system in the spring. Until then, there will be electric heaters in some of the coldest rooms. He has asked students to tell reception if their classroom is cold. "We want everyone to feel comfortable when they are learning," he says.

Many students say this is not enough. They want the college to repair the heating now, before the winter begins.

Task 2

Read Text A and Text B. Answer questions 8–14.

ACold classroom – Room 12To: Mr Patel

Dear Mr Patel,

I am an ESOL student and I study in Room 12 on Mondays and Wednesdays. I am writing because our classroom has been very cold since the end of October.

Most of us wear our coats during the lesson. Last Monday it was so cold that two students went home early. It is very difficult to learn in these conditions.

I read in the college newsletter that the heating will be repaired in the spring. However, the spring is a long time away. Could you please put an electric heater in Room 12 as soon as possible?

I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,
Ahmed Hassan

BRe: Cold classroom – Room 12To: Ahmed Hassan

Dear Ahmed,

Thank you for your email. I am sorry to hear about the problems in Room 12.

I have asked the site team to check the heating in your room this week. We also have two electric heaters, and one of them will be in Room 12 from Thursday.

If the room is still cold after that, please tell your teacher or speak to Linda at reception. She will send me a message.

Thank you for letting us know.

Kind regards,
Raj Patel
College Manager
