In the exam, one question always asks this. Choose the verb that fits the whole text.

What is it mainly about?

  1. Read the title.
  2. Read the first sentence of each paragraph.
  3. Stop. Say the topic in four words or fewer.
  4. Then read everything to check.

The trap: a true detail is not the main point

True, but only a detail

“The shops used to throw food away.”

The main point

“A free weekly dinner run by local people.”

Ask: does this answer cover the whole text, or just one sentence?

Who is “he”? What is “it”?

Rosa has a brother. He lives in Leeds. She visits him every month.

  1. Look back, not forward.
  2. One or more than one? (it / they)
  3. A person or a thing? (he, she / it)
