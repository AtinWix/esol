Many people in Hackney ride a bike to work or college. Cycling is cheap, quick and good for your health. If you do not have a bike, you can borrow one from a community project. Volunteers there repair old bikes and teach new riders how to stay safe on busy roads. Some people join a group ride on Sunday mornings. It is a friendly way to meet neighbours and see new parts of the city.
