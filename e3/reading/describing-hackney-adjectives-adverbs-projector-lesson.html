Adjective or adverb?

What does the word describe?

Adjective → a thing or a place

a busy market

The water is warm.

After be: is, was, are …

Adverb → how we do it

Traders shout loudly.

Ride slowly.

After the action: shout, ride, walk …

quick → quicklycareful → carefullyhappy → happilyeasy → easilygood → wellfast → fasthard → hard
Do: Point to the two boxes, then the words at the bottom.
Say: “This morning we read: ‘Traders shout loudly.’ Shout – HOW? Loudly. That’s an adverb. ‘A busy market’ – busy describes the market. That’s an adjective. … Careful: good becomes WELL. Fast stays fast. Hard stays hard. … A quiet street or a quietly street? Speak quiet or speak quietly?”

PCs · On your own

Find the error

  1. Open Find the error from the Teams post (or scan the code with your phone).
  2. Tap the word that is wrong.
  3. Type the correct sentence.
  4. 20 sentences. Finished? Help your neighbour.

PCs · Online maps

Check your place on the map

  1. Open Google Maps. Search for your place from this morning (A, B, C or D).
  2. Find: the nearest station, and the opening times if it has them.
  3. How long does it take to walk there from college?
  4. Read three reviews. Write down two adjectives people use.
  5. Does the map say the same as your text? Find one thing that is the same and one new thing.
Do: Learners on PCs. After 15 minutes, quick class share.
Say: “Open Google Maps. Search for your place from this morning. Find the nearest station, the opening times, and how long it takes to walk there from college. Read the reviews – find two adjectives. … Ridley Road group – what is the nearest station?”
PCs · Write and post

Write about your favourite place

My favourite place is Victoria Park. It is a large, green park near Hackney Wick. In the morning it is very quiet. I walk slowly round the lake and watch the ducks. There is a lovely café, and the coffee is cheap.

  1. Write 4–5 sentences about a place you like in London.
  2. Use three adjectives and one adverb.
  3. Post it in Teams → General. Start with the name of the place.
Do: Read the example aloud. Point out the highlighted adjectives and the underlined adverb.
Say: “Now write about YOUR favourite place in London. Four or five sentences. Three adjectives and one adverb – like my example. Post it in Teams, General. Start with the name of the place.”
