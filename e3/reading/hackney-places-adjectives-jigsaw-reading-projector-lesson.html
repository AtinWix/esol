Activity 1 · Expert groups

Read and become the expert

  1. Sit in your group: A, B, C or D. Each group has a different place.
  2. Read your text alone first. Then answer questions 1–5.
  3. Check your answers with your group. Find the line in the text that proves each one.
  4. Fill in your row of the grid. You will need it in Activity 2.
Do: Give each table ONE text (A, B, C or D – a copy each) and everyone a grid. Click Start 15 min. Walk round and check answers (day plan, Answers).
Say: “Your table has one place. Read it alone first – no talking. Then answer questions 1 to 5. Then check with your group: find the line that proves each answer. Then write your place in YOUR row of the grid. You are the experts! … Finished? Write one more question about your place for the other groups.”
Activity 2 · New groups

Tell your new group about your place

  1. New groups: one A, one B, one C and one D.
  2. Tell the group about your place. Don’t show your text – talk!
  3. Listen and fill in the grid for the other three places. Ask questions.
It’s in / near …You can …It’s free. / It costs …The best time to go is …It’s very … and …Have you been there?
Do: Make new groups: one A, one B, one C, one D at each table. Click Start 20 min. Point to the useful language.
Say: “Now change tables. Each new group needs an A, a B, a C and a D. Take your grid, not your text. Don’t show your text – TELL them about your place. Everyone else: listen, ask questions and fill in the grid.”

Before you go · Pairs

Tell your partner about a place near you

  1. Where is it?
  2. What can you do or buy there?
  3. Use two adjectives to describe it.

There is a small park near my hostel. It is quiet and green. You can sit and read there, and it is free.

Do: Pairs, 3 minutes. Two or three learners share with the class.
Say: “Tell your partner about a place near where you live. Where is it? What can you do there? Use two adjectives. … Thank you! After the break we go to the PC room.”
