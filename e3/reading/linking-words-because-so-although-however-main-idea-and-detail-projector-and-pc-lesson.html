WHOLE CLASS – eyes on the board

Four words that join ideas

WordIts jobExample
becausegives the reasonI was late because the bus did not come.
sogives the resultThe bus did not come, so I was late.
althougha surprise – in one sentenceAlthough I was late, the teacher was happy.
howevera different idea – new sentence, then a commaI was late. However, I did not miss the test.

Which word?

  1. I was hungry, ___ I made a sandwich.
    I was hungry, so I made a sandwich.
  2. ___ it was Sunday, the shop was open.
    Although it was Sunday, the shop was open.
  3. We stayed inside ___ it was raining.
    We stayed inside because it was raining.
  4. The room is small. ___, it is warm.
    The room is small. However, it is warm.
  5. My bus was late, ___ I missed the start of the class.
    My bus was late, so I missed the start of the class.
  6. ___ she is 70, she cycles every day.
    Although she is 70, she cycles every day.

SAY: “Look at the table. ‘I was late because the bus did not come.’ Which part is the reason? (The bus did not come.) ‘The bus did not come, so I was late.’ Which part is the result? (I was late.)” SAY: “Although and however show a surprise or a different idea. Although is inside one sentence. However starts a new sentence, and there is a comma after it.” DO: Which word? – read each sentence with ‘beep’ for the gap; the class calls the word; a learner says the full sentence; THEN click (one answer per click). Answers: so · Although · because · However · so · Although. Learners look at: the projector.

Task B · Read and answer – the point counts only if your first click is right

Should the college library open in the evening?

1 The library at a college in east London is open from 9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday. Some learners want it to stay open until 8pm. We asked three people for their opinion.

2 Fatima studies English in the morning. “I have two young children, so I cannot study at home in the evening,” she says. “However, I do not need the library at night because I finish my homework there before lunch. Although evening opening is a good idea for other people, it will not help me.”

3 Daniel works in a warehouse until 5pm. Then he comes to college for his English class at 6pm. “I can never use the library because it closes before I arrive,” he says. “My flat is small and noisy, so I need a quiet place to study. Please open in the evening!”

4 Grace has worked in the library for ten years. “I understand the problem. However, the college needs more staff for evening opening, so it will cost more money,” she says. “Although I want to help learners like Daniel, I cannot work every evening.”

5 What do you think?

Find the mistake

One mistake in each sentence. Tell your partner – then tell the class.

  1. I stayed at home so I was ill.
    I stayed at home because I was ill.
  2. Although it was late, but he finished his homework.
    Although it was late, he finished his homework. (no ‘but’)
  3. The bus is slow. However it is cheap.
    The bus is slow. However, it is cheap. (comma)
  4. Because the shop was closed, so I went home.
    Because the shop was closed, I went home. (no ‘so’)
  5. He was hungry because he ate two sandwiches.
    He was hungry, so he ate two sandwiches.
  6. She likes tea, however she does not like coffee.
    She likes tea. However, she does not like coffee. (new sentence)

  Before you go: tell your partner one true sentence with although.

SAY: “Eyes on the board. Each sentence has one mistake. Number 1 – talk to your partner for 20 seconds.” DO: a learner says the correct sentence; THEN click (one per click). Read two good Teams posts aloud. FINISH: “Tell your partner one true sentence with although.” Hear three. SAY: “On Wednesday we use these words to find the main point in exam texts.” Learners look at: the projector.
