Grammar: comparatives

short wordssweet → sweeter   nice → nicer
one vowel + consonanthot → hotter   big → bigger
ends in -ytasty → tastier   healthy → healthier
long wordsmore delicious   more expensive
differentgood → better   bad → worse

Cheese was worse than chocolate.

Go through the rules row by row; elicit one more example for each. Drill “sweeter than”, “worse than” (weak /ðən/). Learners look at: the projector. 10 minutes.

Task 5 · Practice

Forms

  1. sweet · nice · hot · tasty
    sweeter · nicer · hotter · tastier
  2. healthy · delicious · good · bad
    healthier · more delicious · better · worse

Sentences

  1. For Maurice, cheese was ___ (bad) chocolate.
    For Maurice, cheese was worse than chocolate.
  2. Maurice thinks Ugwumpiti was ___ (good) any other food.
    Maurice thinks Ugwumpiti was better than any other food.
  3. For Maurice, a roasted rat was ___ (nice) cheese!
    For Maurice, a roasted rat was nicer than cheese!
  4. London was ___ (safe) Biafra in the war.
    London was safer than Biafra in the war.
  5. Fruit is ___ (healthy) chocolate.
    Fruit is healthier than chocolate.
  6. Food in the UK is ___ (expensive) food in my country.
    Food in the UK is more expensive than food in my country.

E2: forms + sentences 1–4. E3: all. 12 minutes, then check: a learner reads, then click. Number 6 is the learner’s opinion – the form is “more expensive than”. Learners look at: worksheet Task 5, then the projector. 15 minutes.

Maurice’s story · Find the mistake

There is one mistake in each sentence. Tell your partner – then tell the class.

  1. Maurice born in Biafra, in Nigeria, in 1961.
    Maurice was born in Biafra, in Nigeria, in 1961.
  2. His parents left Maurice and his brother with thier grandmothers.
    His parents left Maurice and his brother with their grandmothers.
  3. When Maurice was five, a war start in Biafra.
    When Maurice was five, a war started in Biafra.
  4. During the war, the children often queue for food from the Red Cross.
    During the war, the children often queued for food from the Red Cross.
  5. After the war end, Maurice and his brother came to London.
    After the war ended, Maurice and his brother came to London.
  6. They didn’t spoke English.
    They didn’t speak English.
  7. School in Hackney was very dificult for them.
    School in Hackney was very difficult for them.
  8. The cars in London were more fast than the cars in Biafra.
    The cars in London were faster than the cars in Biafra.

From your “Ugwumpiti reading and questions” text. Give pairs 30 seconds per sentence (or all 8 at once for E3). A learner says the correct sentence, then click. Seven are past simple or spelling; number 8 recycles the comparatives. Learners look at: the projector. 10 minutes.
