Grammar: adjectives

very / really + normal adjective

very cold · really nice · very dirty · very beautiful

absolutely / really + strong adjective

absolutely freezing · absolutely fantastic · really terrible

✗ very fantastic   ✓ absolutely fantastic

Strong adjectives already mean “very”. Really goes with both. Drill with exaggerated intonation: “It was ABsolutely FREEzing!” Learners look at: the projector. 10 minutes.

Task 5 · Practice

Part A · Good 😊 or bad ☹?

fantastic · awful · beautiful · dirty · exciting · terrible · colourful · boring · wonderful · horrible

  1. 😊 good
    fantastic, beautiful, exciting, colourful, wonderful
  2. ☹ bad
    awful, dirty, terrible, boring, horrible

Part B · very or absolutely?

  1. The winter in 1964 was ___ cold.
    The winter in 1964 was very cold.
  2. It was ___ freezing!
    It was absolutely freezing!
  3. Manuhar thought the airport was ___ fantastic.
    Manuhar thought the airport was absolutely fantastic.
  4. The house was ___ dirty.
    The house was very dirty.
  5. The lights were ___ beautiful.
    The lights were very beautiful.
  6. His new home was ___ terrible.
    His new home was absolutely terrible.

really is correct in all six.

E2: Part A + Part B 1–3. E3: all. 10 minutes, then check: a learner reads, then click. Learners look at: worksheet Task 5, then the projector. 15 minutes.
