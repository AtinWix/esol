Grammar: past simple

Regular: wait → waited · stay → stayed · rescue → rescued

Irregular: leave → left · get → got · find → found · throw → threw · go → went · have → had

Negative: They didn’t have any food.

✗ didn’t had

Linh left Vietnam when she was seven.

Regular -ed has three sounds: /t/ (stopped), /d/ (stayed), /ɪd/ (waited). Drill the irregular verbs. Ask: “Did they have food?” – “No, they didn’t.” Learners look at: the projector. 10 minutes.

Task 5 · Practice

Verbs

  1. wait · stay · rescue · arrive
    waited · stayed · rescued · arrived
  2. leave · get · find · throw
    left · got · found · threw
  3. go · have · see · take
    went · had · saw · took

Linh’s story

  1. When she was seven, Linh ___ (leave) Vietnam with her father.
    When she was seven, Linh left Vietnam with her father.
  2. First, they ___ (wait) in the water.
    First, they waited in the water.
  3. Then they ___ (get) on a tiny boat.
    Then they got on a tiny boat.
  4. After a few days, they ___ (not have) any food.
    After a few days, they didn’t have any food.
  5. Then a British ship ___ (find) them.
    Then a British ship found them.
  6. The sailors ___ (throw) down food.
    The sailors threw down food.
  7. After that, they ___ (stay) in a camp in Singapore.
    After that, they stayed in a camp in Singapore.
  8. Finally, they ___ (go) to Britain.
    Finally, they went to Britain.

E2: verb table + gaps 1–5. E3: all. 12 minutes on the worksheet, then check: a learner reads, then click. Learners look at: worksheet Task 5, then the projector. 15 minutes.
