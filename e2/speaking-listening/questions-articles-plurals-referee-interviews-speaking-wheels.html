2 How to play: Referee Interviews

🙋 A – asks

Spin a wheel. Ask your partner questions.

Fast!

💬 B – answers

Answer in full sentences.

Hear a slip? Fix it and say the whole sentence again.

🧑‍⚖️ C – referee

Listen. Show 1, 2 or 3 fingers.

Don't say the answer. The speaker finds it.

Points

Speaker fixes the mistake → 1 point to the speaker.

Referee catches a mistake → 1 point to the referee.

Teacher hears a mistake nobody caught → 1 point to the teacher!

Change roles every 3 minutes. Everyone plays A, B and C.
