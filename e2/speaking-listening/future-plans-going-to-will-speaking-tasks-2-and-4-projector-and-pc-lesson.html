going to – plans

We use be + going to + verb for plans. We decided before now.

✅ Positive❌ Negative❓ QuestionShort answers
II'm going to cook.I'm not going to cook.Am I going to cook?Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
he / she / itShe's going to cook.She isn't going to cook.Is she going to cook?Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
you / we / theyThey're going to cook.They aren't going to cook.Are they going to cook?Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

❓ What are you going to do?   Where is he going to go?   When are they going to start?

⏰ tonight · tomorrow · on Saturday · this weekend · next week · next month · next year

SAY: "Plan. You decided yesterday, last week. Now you say: I'm going to…" Drill chorally: "I'm going to cook. She's going to cook. They're going to cook." Then negatives and questions. Point at the verb: "going to + verb – no -s, no -ed, no -ing: She's going to cook, NOT cooks." Ask 4 learners: "What are you going to do tonight?" – insist on full sentences.

will – decide now, promise, predict

1. Decide now (no plan before – this includes offers to help)
"The bus is late." "I'll walk."
"This bag is heavy." "I'll help you."
2. Promise
"I'll call you tonight."
"Don't worry. I won't be late."
3. Predict – your opinion (I think… / probably)
I think it'll be cold tomorrow.
But you can SEE it now (black clouds) → going to: It's going to rain.

I'll = I will · it'll = it will · I won't = I will not · will + verb (no -s): She will help.

Vote: A = going to   B = will

  1. I have a ticket. I ___ visit Leeds on Friday. A – going to (plan)
    I have a ticket. I'm going to visit Leeds on Friday.
  2. "I'm cold." "I ___ close the window." B – will (decide now – an offer)
    "I'm cold." "I'll close the window."
  3. "Can you pay me back?" "Yes, I ___ pay you on Friday. I promise." B – will (promise)
    "Can you pay me back?" "Yes, I'll pay you on Friday. I promise."
  4. We ___ move flat next month. We signed the papers. A – going to (plan)
    We're going to move flat next month. We signed the papers.
  5. I think Ali ___ pass his exam. He works hard. B – will (prediction – my opinion)
    I think Ali will pass his exam. He works hard.
  6. Look at those black clouds! It ___ rain. A – going to (I can see it now)
    Look at those black clouds! It's going to rain.
  7. "The lift is broken." "OK, I ___ take the stairs." B – will (decide now)
    "The lift is broken." "OK, I'll take the stairs."
  8. She ___ start college in January. She has a place. A – going to (plan)
    She's going to start college in January. She has a place.
